_Games for Saturday, November 2, 2019. All times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located._

11 am: Slim pickings. Go for whatever is competitive between Boston College at Syracuse (ACCN), Houston at UCF (ESPN2), Michigan at Maryland (ABC), NC State at Wake Forest (ESPN) or Nebraska at Purdue (FOX). At 11:30 but probably in some obscure internet stream is a big Patriot League showdown between Holy Cross and Lehigh.

1:30 pm: Virginia Tech at Notre Dame (NBC) to see which direction both coaches’ fortunes start to go.

2:30 pm: Georgia vs Florida (CBS) where the stark contrast of play-calling skills will be on display. Kirby Smart learning the DiNardo-Miles Principle of promoting inexperienced coordinators. Clickover to K-State at Kansas (FS1). Also Miami at Florida State (ABC) if you like to watch slow car crashes.

6:30 pm: Virginia at North Carolina (ACCN) to check in on the Mack Brown project, followed by Oregon at USC (FOX, 7pm) and Colorado at UCLA (Pac12, 8 pm).

Pac12 After Dark: BYU at Utah State (ESPN2), Boise State at San Jose State (CBSSN) and New Mexico at Nevada (ESPNU).
