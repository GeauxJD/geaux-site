Today’s menu: Gator Sausage, Boudin & Burgers

Games for Saturday, October 12, 2019. All times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located.

11 am: Oklahoma vs Texas (Fox) where the Longhorns will need to park that giant cowboy statue from the state fair in front of the Cotton Bowl end zone to have any hopes of stopping the Sooners from scoring. Clickover to a sneaky-good Memphis at Temple (ESPN2) matchup because South Carolina at Georgia (ESPN) won’t be competitive.

2:30 pm: Mid afternoon is a great time to do other things because Alabama will blow out Texas A&M (CBS), Clemson will blow out Florida State (ABC) and Michigan State at Wisconsin (BTN) will set offensive football back by 4 decades. The Fighting Mike Leaches at the Fighting Herm Edwardses could be fun for the tens of people who get the Pac12 network.

6:30 pm: USC at Notre Dame (NBC) and Penn State at Iowa (ABC) give us 30 pregame minutes and then halftime clickover fare for…

7 pm: Florida at LSU (ESPN). Imagine Joe Alleva’s surprise when he tunes in from the 19th hole after a hard day of special consultant fundraising and sees the Gators playing a football game in Baton Rouge and not Gainesville. A toast to you, Hothead Joe!

Pac12 After Dark: Hawaii at Boise State (ESPN2, 9:15 pm), Wyoming at San Diego State (CBSSN, 9:30 pm) and Washington at Arizona (FS1, 10 pm) is a nice staggered start of potentially interesting games … if we don’t have to hate football by then because of the evening’s results.
