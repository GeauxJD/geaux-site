11 am: Iowa at Iowa State (Fox) Does Kirk Ferentz own a cell phone? Pretty sure he uses the word “newfangled” at least three times a week. Clickovers to Baylor at SMU (CW), Virginia at NC State (ESPN2) and Illinois at Duke (ESPN).

2:30 pm: Oklahoma State at Oregon (CBS) How much practice time did Mike Gundy spend preaching to his players about caution around the lawless people on the mean streets of Portland this week? Clickovers to Kansas at Missouri (ESPN2), Ole Miss at Kentucky (ABC) and Fresno State at Oregon State (CW).

6:30 pm: Michigan at Oklahoma (ABC) Let’s see which version of buying a quarterback is better. Clickovers to Arizona State at MooU (ESPN2), Vanderbilt at Va Tech (ACCN) and UCLA at UNLV (CBSSN).

Sark After Dark: San Diego State at Washington State (CW), Stanford at BYU (ESPN) and UC Davis at Washington (BTN). Yes, that’s not a typo. UC Davis at Washington on the Big Ten Network, a very normal thing in the year 2025.
