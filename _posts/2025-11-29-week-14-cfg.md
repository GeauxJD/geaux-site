Friday

11 am: Ole Miss at MooU (ABC) Imagine being a Mississippi State fan and having to endure a four hour broadcast about Lane Kiffin’s coaching future. Clickovers to Iowa at Nebraska (CBS) and Ohio at Buffalo (ESPNU).

2:30 pm: Georgia at Georgia Tech (ABC) Two weeks ago the Jackets had playoff and conference championship game hopes. Today they just get whomped by the Dawgs again. Clickovers to Boise State at Utah State (CBS), San Diego State at New Mexico (CBSSN) and Temple at North Texas (ESPN).
