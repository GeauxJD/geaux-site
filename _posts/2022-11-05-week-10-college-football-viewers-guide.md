11 am: The early slate is a jumbled pile of "wow they really fell off a cliff" teams squaring off, leading with Florida at Texas A&M (ESPN) and Iowa at Purdue (FS1). Maryland at Wisconsin (BTN) and Minnesota at Nebraska (ESPN2) might be competitive?
