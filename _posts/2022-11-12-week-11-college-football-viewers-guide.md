_(all times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located)_

11 am: LSU at Arkansas (ESPN) It’s a must-win for the Tigers. Not because of the potential SEC Championship game bid, but because nobody wants to hand a transitive win to Liberty. Clickovers to Oklahoma at West Virginia (FS1) and Purdue at Illinois (ESPN2).

2:30 pm: Alabama at Ole Miss (CBS) No coach has handed Nick Saban a third loss in a season since 2010. The Lane Train thinks he can, he thinks he can. Clickovers to Louisville at Clemson (ESPN) and UCF at Tulane (ESPN2).

6:30 pm: Texas A&M at Auburn (SECN) Sure, K-State at Baylor (FS1) is a more important game for conference standings and whatnot, but who can resist the potential for mutual disaster between these Aggie and War Eagle Plainsmen Tiger clubs? Clickovers to TCU at Texas (ABC), North Carolina at Wake Forest (ESPN2) and Washington at Oregon (Fox).

Sark After Dark: San Jose State at San Diego State (FS1), Arizona at UCLA (Fox), Boise State at Nevada (CBSSN) and Stanford at Utah (ESPN).
