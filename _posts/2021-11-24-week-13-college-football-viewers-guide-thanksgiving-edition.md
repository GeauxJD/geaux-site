11 am: Boise State at San Diego State (CBS) would qualify as sneaky good except it’s on CBS and not streaming. Also check on how much Texas has quit against K-State (Fox). Iowa at Nebraska (12:30 pm, BTN) if you like farms and UTEP at UAB (1 pm, ESPN+) for a sneaky good streaming game.

11 am: Ohio State at Michigan (Fox) Have you heard they call it The Big Game?! Florida State at Florida (ESPN) is a battle for bowl eligibility, while Wake Forest at Boston College (espn2) and Texas Tech at Baylor (FS1) have conference championship games on the line.

2:30 pm: Penn State at Michigan State (ABC) in the ”Oh my God how much did we just commit to these coaches in contracts?” bowl. Clickovers to Oregon State at Oregon (ESPN) and Wisconsing at Minnesota (3 pm, Fox).

6:30 pm: Oklahoma at Oklahoma State (ABC) in what could be the best Bedlam game in ages. Clickover to the end of the Coach O era vs Texas A&M (6 pm, ESPN) and Pitt at Syracuse (ACCN).

Sark After Dark: BYU at USC (ESPN) and Cal at UCLA (FS1).
