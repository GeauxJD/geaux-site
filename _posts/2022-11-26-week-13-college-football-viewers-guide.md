The world’s foremost authority on college football viewership, helping tens of people to clickover from Bama blowouts to more quality games since 2018!

_(all times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located)_

11 am: Michigan at Ohio State (Fox) The Buckeyes like to cover up the letter M on all campus signs for this game. Which turns the Mass Comm building into something very different. Clickovers to Georgia Tech at Georgia (ESPN), Coastal Carolina at James Madison (ESPNU) and South Carolina at Clemson (ABC).

2:30 pm: Auburn at Alabama (CBS) Hugh Freeze the Super Genius and Auburn are a match made in … somewhere. Also, Iowa State at TCU (Fox, 3 pm) has the Frogs trying to continue their perplexing unbeaten run. Meanwhile a trio of games has the Big Ten West feeling like the final group stage matches at the World Cup: Purdue at Indiana (BTN), Minnesota at Wisconsin (ESPN) and Illinois at Northwestern (BTN).

6 pm: LSU at Texas A&M (ESPN) leads into an evening slate with various things on the line including Kansas trying to spoil K-State’s Big 12 championship game bid (Fox, 7 pm) and Vanderbilt needing to beat Tennessee at home to earn an improbable bowl trip (SECN, 6:30 pm). Clickovers include Notre Dame at USC (ABC), Oklahoma at Texas Tech (FS1) and Pittsburgh at Miami (ACCN).

Sark After Dark: Washington at Washington State (ESPN) The Apple Cup in Pullman is as Sark After Dark as it gets. Clickovers: BYU at Stanford (FS1) and SEMO at Montana (ESPN2).
