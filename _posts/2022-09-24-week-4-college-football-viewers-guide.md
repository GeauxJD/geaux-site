11 am: Duke at Kansas (FS1) for undefeated glory. The winner gets to look over at Nebraska and laugh and laugh. Clickovers to Clemson at Wake (ABC), Baylor at Iowa State (ESPN2) and TCU at SMU (ESPNU).

2:30 pm: Florida at Tennessee (CBS) Are we ready for a world where Tennessee is competitive again? If not don’t worry, this will only be an open question until next month when the Vols get wallopped by Bama yet again. Clickovers to Notre Dame at North Carolina (ABC) and Texas at Texas Tech (ESPN).

6:30 pm: Wisconsin at Ohio State (ABC) for lots of points or Iowa at Rutgers (FS1) for lots of punts. For lots of overtime (as is their tradition), clickover to Arkansas vs. Texas A&M (ESPN). 

Sark After Dark: Utah at Arizona State (ESPN) to see how the Sun Devils respond to being Herm-free. Also Wyoming at BYU (ESPN2), Stanford at Washington (FS1) and Western Michigan at San Jose State (CBSSN).
