_(all times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located)_

11 am: TCU at Baylor (Fox) and Illinois at Michigan (ABC) have a pair of capable teams attempting to derail playoff hopes if you are interested in such things. A few deeper cuts that might offer various forms of entertainment include Florida at Vanderbilt (SECN), UConn at Army (CBSSN), Wisconsin at Nebraska (ESPN) and Yale at Harvard (ESPNU).

2:30 pm: Texas at Kansas (FS1) The Longhorns are good at losing to bad Kansas teams, so how will Bevo handle things against a surprisingly decent version of the Jayhawks? Clickovers to Miami at Clemson (ESPN), Iowa at Minnesota (Fox) and Georgia at Kentucky (CBS).

6 pm: Tennessee at South Carolina (ESPN) to see if Beamer Ball Junior can set a trap for the Vols. To catch Heupel we’re guessing it’s a cardboard box perched on a stick with some Golden Corral gift cards underneath. In case of blowout there’s also USC at UCLA (Fox) kicking off an hour later. Clickovers to Ole Miss at Arkansas (SECN), Oklahoma State at Oklahoma (ABC) and Texas Tech at Iowa State (FS1).

Sark After Dark: UAB at LSU (ESPN2) starts at 8 pm for some reason, followed by San Jose State at Utah State (FS1), Fresno State at Nevada (CBSSN) and Utah at Oregon (ESPN).
