11 am: Kentucky at Ole Miss (ESPN) feels like it deserves primetime billing. In the Big Ten Michigan at Iowa (Fox) and Purdue at Minnesota (ESPN2) are both pretty big blowout risks. Oklahoma at TCU (ABC) could be competitive if the Big 12 is your thing. Clickover to Georgia State at Army (CBSSN) for the foliage of course.

2:30 pm: Oklahoma State at Baylor (Fox) and Iowa State at Kansas (ESPN2) claim the afternoon window for the Big 12, because Alabama at Arkansas (CBS) has all the makings of a boring Bama blowout. Clickover to Texas A&M at Mississippi State (SECN) if you enjoy maroon a whole lot.

6 pm: LSU at Auburn (ESPN) - Brian Harsin got bailed out last weekend by a series of unfortunate Mizzou events. Will the infamous Auburn Refs make a visit to The Plains this weekend to bail him out again? If so it would be the world’s most unhappy 4-1 fan base since some point during the Les Miles era in Baton Rouge. Clickovers to Cincinnati at Tulsa (ESPNU) and NC State at Clemson (ABC).

Sark After Dark: Stanford at Oregon (FS1), Arizona State at USC (ESPN) and UC Davis at Montana State (ESPNU).
