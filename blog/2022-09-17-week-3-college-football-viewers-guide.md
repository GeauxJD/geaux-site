The world’s foremost authority on college football viewership, helping tens of people to clickover from Bama blowouts to more quality games since 2018!

_(all times CT because that’s where the SEC West is located)_

11 am: Georgia at South Carolina (ESPN) for the nostalgia of those Mark Richt losing control trips of yore to Columbia. Once it’s certain that isn’t happening, clickovers to Oklahoma at Nebraska (Fox) and Purdue at Syracuse (ESPN2).

2:30 pm: A trio of options here including Penn State at Auburn (CBS), BYU at Oregon (Fox) and Ole Miss at Georgia Tech (ABC). In case all of those are duds there’s even a solid trio of clickovers including Cal at Notre Dame (1:30 pm, NBC), Colorado at Minnesota (ESPN2) and Kansas at Houston (3 pm, ESPNU).

5 pm: MooU at LSU (ESPN) Imagine telling somebody in 2015 that Mike Leach vs. Brian Kelly would be an SEC West mid-table showdown.

8 pm: Miami at Texas A&M (ESPN) We’d make a joke about the culture shock of Miami fans visiting the metroplex that is Bryan-College Station, but this would require Miami to have fans who attend games in person.

Sark After Dark: Fresno State at USC (Fox), San Diego State at Utah (ESPN2) and North Dakota State at Arizona (FS1). There’s also the adventure of finding Duquesne at Hawaii at 11 pm on an obscure streaming app.
